Jump Rope Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Who is responsible

Daniel Barton publishes Jump Rope Tracker and handles questions about this policy. The app works without a Jump Rope Tracker account. You can reach us using the contact link below.

Camera and pose tracking

With camera permission, Google MediaPipe analyzes frames on your device to estimate body landmarks and count jumps. The app uses live frames for tracking and discards them after processing. The workout view can request fonts, tracking code, and models from Google and jsDelivr. These services receive ordinary network request information such as your IP address. Google says MediaPipe may send performance and usage metrics, such as inference counts, device information, and general input characteristics, for diagnostics and service improvement. See the MediaPipe links below.

Online legal documents

When you open a legal document in the app, the app requests the published copy from GitHub Pages. GitHub receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address to serve the page.

Workouts and settings

The app stores your chosen display name, optional weigh-ins, workouts, goals, preferences, reminders, and legal acknowledgments on your device. Workout entries can contain dates and times, counts and streaks, estimated distance and energy, weight used in estimates, heart rate, and multiplayer results. The app uses this information to show workout history, calculate estimates, restore workouts, and provide features you enable, including reminders and widgets.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

With your permission on supported devices, the app uses HealthKit to read heart rate and active energy and to save jump rope workouts and active energy. Apple Watch can use motion data to count jumps and exchange workout progress, history, and workout summaries with its paired iPhone. The app uses health and fitness information for workout features and keeps it separate from advertising. Manage Health access in Apple Health or your device settings.

Multiplayer

When you join nearby play or Apple SharePlay, the app exchanges your display name, workout settings, and live results such as jump counts and elapsed time with participants so you can play together. Workout history can retain another player’s name and jump count. Each participant manages the copy on their device.

Ads and purchases

When ads are enabled on a supported device, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process IP address, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. Advertising may depend on regional requirements and your choices. Where offered, manage choices under Home → ⋯ → Advanced Settings → Ad privacy choices. The app keeps camera frames, workout history, and Health data separate from AdMob. Apple handles in-app purchases and provides the app with purchase entitlement information to restore access.

Retention, deletion, and permissions

Saved workouts and settings remain on the device until you delete them. Delete individual entries in History or use Home → ⋯ → Advanced Settings → Erase All App Data. Manage camera, Health, local network, motion, and notification access in your device settings. Erasing app data leaves copies you exported, records in Apple Health, and copies on other devices. Manage those copies where they are stored. Exported SQLite backups can contain workout and settings data. You control where they are saved and who can access them.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and keep correspondence as needed to resolve your request and meet legal obligations. Use the contact link below to ask about this policy or to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you.
